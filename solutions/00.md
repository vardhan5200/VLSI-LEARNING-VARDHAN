## Exercise 1

13 = 8+4+1 = `1101`. Unsigned: 13. Signed: −8+4+1 = −3. Negating `0011` by inversion plus one also produces `1101`.

## Exercise 2

For S=0, the four A,B combinations 00,01,10,11 produce Y=0,0,1,1. For S=1 they produce Y=0,1,0,1. When S=0, S'=1 and SB=0, leaving A; when S=1, S'A=0 and SB=B.

## Exercise 3

Starting Q=0, enabled edges produce 1,2,3,4,5,6. The two disabled edges both leave Q=6. An additional two enabled edges would produce 7,0 because the register has only three bits.
