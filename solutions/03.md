## Exercise 1

After the first edge a=0,b=1. After the second, a=1,b=0. They alternate because each gets the other's old value.

## Exercise 2

The missing en=0 assignment requires q to retain its previous value, so a latch can be inferred. A combinational alternative is `always @* q = en ? d : fallback;`. It selects a current input instead of holding history.

## Exercise 3

First increment to a nonzero value, then drive rst=1 and en=1 before a rising edge. Q must become 0 on that edge, because the specification gives reset priority. The supplied testbench includes this case. A stronger mutation experiment is to reverse the reset/enable priority and confirm that this test fails.
