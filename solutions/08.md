## Exercise 1

10−2−1=7 ns. This simplified input-to-register budget excludes clock skew, uncertainty, and other delays; those consume or alter margin in a real calculation.

## Exercise 2

Maximum input delay supports latest-arrival/setup analysis. Minimum input delay supports earliest-arrival/hold analysis. Supplying only a maximum can misrepresent the environment or leave the intended minimum unspecified, depending on tool semantics.

## Exercise 3

A valid exception needs architectural/protocol evidence that the specified launch/capture relationship cannot or need not occur, plus exact endpoint selection and review of other relevant checks. If the path is used every cycle, fix timing through logic, cells, placement, buffering, or an explicitly redesigned pipeline/protocol. An exception that merely makes reports green is not a fix.
