Basic Concepts of Integrated Circuit - I

Based on the retrieved timestamped transcript
3:22 ↗

Historical perspective

Connecting separate components becomes costly and difficult as circuits grow. Integration forms many components on one silicon substrate.

8:20 ↗

Scaling

The lecture introduces Moore’s prediction and explains why shrinking devices increases integration while making design more complex.

12:07 ↗

Structure

Devices lie below interconnect layers. Different wiring planes allow connections that would otherwise cross and short.

21:09 ↗

Photolithography

A mask controls light exposure. Development, etching and resist removal transfer its pattern to the film; repeated use enables copying.

29:06 ↗

Fabrication terms

The lecture distinguishes ingot, wafer, die and packaged chip, and introduces yield as the proportion of good dies.

37:00 ↗

Industry roles

Design specifies the circuit; fabrication produces it. Fabless firms, foundries and IDMs organize these roles differently. PDKs communicate process information and rules.
