Attempt every exercise first. An answer is complete only when you explain why it follows.

## Exercise 1

The modeled switching power halves from 0.4 to 0.2 mW. Total chip power may drop by less because leakage and other power components need not halve.

## Exercise 2

Each balanced chain contains 100 cells, so loading needs 100 clocks if all chains shift in parallel. Test access must provide/collect parallel streams, directly or through compression/decompression logic. More chains are not free: routing, pins/access, and test infrastructure matter.

## Exercise 3

Clock-gate idle registers with an appropriate integrated gating approach; verify enable behavior, wake-up timing, test bypass, and clock checks. Power-gate a suitable domain only with an explicit plan for isolation, state retention or reinitialization, power sequencing, and wake-up behavior. Compare overhead against actual idle duration.
