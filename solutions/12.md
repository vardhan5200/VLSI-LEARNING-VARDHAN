## Exercise 2

Possible causes are an open connection or a short between nets despite legal local geometries; a device or pin mapping mismatch is another possibility. Passing DRC does not establish connectivity or tape-out readiness. Diagnose the LVS mismatch and repeat affected checks after fixing it.
