## Exercise 2

R doubles and C doubles, so RC becomes four times larger. This is an approximate unbuffered wire model. Buffering, changes in layer geometry, and coupling can alter real behavior.

## Exercise 3

The observed response compressed to the expected signature for the applied sequence and implemented check. It does not prove every possible behavior or defect was exercised, and aliasing can hide a differing response. Also verify BIST controller operation and the validity of the expected signature.
